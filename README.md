My aim is to make IT straightforward and dependable, helping businesses resolve technical problems, maintain their systems and networks, and get on with what they do best.

## 🦔 Caffoot — Infrastructure, Simplified.

I'm also the creator of **[Caffoot](https://github.com/Caffoot)**, an open-source project focused on making infrastructure management more accessible.

**[Caffoot Lite](https://github.com/Caffoot/Caffoot-Lite)** is a free, offline application for IP address management (IPAM), network inventory, IT asset tracking and rack management.

I'm also exploring the future of **Caffoot Pro**, building on the design and functionality of Lite.

[Explore Caffoot Lite](https://github.com/Caffoot/Caffoot-Lite) · [Download the latest release](https://github.com/Caffoot/Caffoot-Lite/releases/latest)
